# phantom-track.com

This is the Next.js 16 marketing and shop site for Phantom Track. Current state and open work are in `HANDOFF.md`.

## Owner's standing rules

The owner has asked for these more than once. Follow them every session.

**Committing and branches**
- **Finish the loop.** When work is done, commit it and push it. Don't leave uncommitted changes lying around: keep them, delete them, or push them. Stop to ask only when the decision really is the owner's.
- **Commit as the owner.** Commits are by the owner only: `allen <mocinjay1979@icloud.com>`. Add no `Co-Authored-By` or "Generated with" lines.
- **Branches.** Work on `site-cleanup`. `main` is production on Vercel, so merge or push to `main` only when the owner says to.

**Visual work**
- **Real and professional.**
  - Use real device frames.
  - Show Android screenshots in a Pixel frame and iOS screenshots in an iPhone frame.
  - Give the pages depth (angled phones), not flat screenshots.
  - Nothing should look faked, see-through or bland.
- **Show your work.** The owner judges by eye. After a visual change, take a screenshot and give a localhost link.
- **Fast pages.** Ship pre-compressed WebP with `unoptimized` for large images.

**Talking to the owner**
- Reply in chat, and keep it short.
- Say plainly what is done and what you checked.
